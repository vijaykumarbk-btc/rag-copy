# Query: requirements for Adjacent Segment Disease

*Generated: 2026-08-18 16:38:05 | mode=hybrid | alpha=0.5 | top_k=5*

## Answer

According to the guidelines, Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) for a degenerative spinal segment adjacent to a previous decompression or fusion procedure is considered medically necessary when performed for **Radiculopathy** and ALL of the following criteria are met [3]:

*   **Timing:** It has been greater than six (6) months since the prior cervical decompression or cervical fusion at an adjacent level [3].
*   **Symptoms:** The patient experiences BOTH of the following [3]:
    *   Significant level of pain on a daily basis defined as clinically significant functional impairment (e.g., inability to perform household chores, prolonged standing, etc.) [3].
    *   Unremitting radicular pain to the shoulder girdle and/or upper extremity resulting in disability [3].
*   **Physical Exam Findings:** The exam includes ANY of the following [3]:
    *   Dermatomal sensory deficit [3].
    *   Motor deficit (e.g., biceps, triceps weakness) [3].
    *   Reflex changes [3].
    *   Shoulder abduction relief sign [3].
    *   Nerve root tension sign (e.g., Spurling's maneuver) [3].
    *   Unremitting radicular pain to the shoulder girdle and/or upper extremity without concordant objective physical exam findings [3].
*   **Conservative Treatment:** There is less than clinically meaningful improvement with at least TWO of the following (unless contraindicated) [3]:
    *   Prescription strength analgesics, steroids, gabapentinoids, and/or nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) for six (6) weeks [3].
    *   Provider-directed exercise program (prescribed by a physical therapist, chiropractic provider, osteopathic or allopathic physician) for six (6) weeks [3].
    *   Epidural steroid injection(s) or selective nerve root block(s) performed at the same level(s) as the requested surgery [3].
*   **Imaging (Previous Level):** Plain x-rays of the cervical spine (including flexion/extension lateral views) and advanced diagnostic imaging show successful decompression and fusion at the previous operative level [3].
*   **Imaging (Requested Level):** MRI/CT shows neural structure compression at the requested level(s) that is concordant with the individual's symptoms and physical exam findings and is caused by adjacent segment disease [3].

Additionally, the determination of medical necessity is always made on a case-by-case basis [2]. For additional timing and documentation requirements, one should refer to CMM-600.1: Prior Authorization Requirements [2].
